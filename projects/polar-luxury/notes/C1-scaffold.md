# C1 — 5-Beat Scaffold

PROJECT: Polar Bear × Heritage Winter Luxury · IG Reel · 30 sec
CONCEPT: A polar bear wearing a Burberry-style cashmere check scarf — winter heritage luxury that no real shoot could legally, physically, or ethically execute.

BEAT 1 — Setup (0–6s)
  · The impossible question:    "This shoot wouldn't happen."
  · The visual:                 Polar bear walks out of falling snow into a cream-and-camel studio space.
                                Stops dead-center. Holds eye contact with camera.
                                One soft paw thud on snow-dusted floor.
  · Role in arc:                state the impossible

BEAT 2 — Escalation 1 (6–12s)
  · First reason it can't happen:  "No carrier will insure it."
  · The visual:                    Push-in to neck and chest. A camel-cream tartan cashmere
                                   scarf (no brand wordmark) drapes over the bear's neck,
                                   catches soft key light. Head tilts slightly left.
  · Role in arc:                   reveal #1 — financial impossibility

BEAT 3 — Escalation 2 (12–18s)
  · Second reason — bigger:        "No house would risk the product."
  · The visual:                    Macro slow-mo of front paw on cream marble. Black leather
                                   trench-coat cuff (scale-correct for the paw — paw is ~4×
                                   the cuff button) drapes over the leg. Snow dust falls
                                   through key light.
  · Role in arc:                   reveal #2 — the unfakeable shot

BEAT 4 — Peak (18–24s)
  · Third reason — most dramatic:  "And no crew would survive the booking."
  · The visual:                    Head-and-shoulders portrait. Round tortoiseshell-frame
                                   aviator sunglasses sit on the bear's snout. Predator stare
                                   into lens. Light fur breath visible. Whiskers twitch.
  · Role in arc:                   reveal #3 — peak physical impossibility

BEAT 5 — Landing (24–30s)
  · Defiance + brand reveal:       "But it just did.
                                   AI Production Club.
                                   Studio shoots no studio could shoot."
  · The visual:                    Pull-back to wide. Bear sits on cream snow set. Full ensemble
                                   visible — scarf, coat cuff, sunglasses, and a small camel
                                   structured handbag at the paw. APC wordmark fades in gold
                                   on cream-warm-black.
  · Role in arc:                   resolution + signature

VARIETY OF IMPOSSIBILITIES (Rule 1 check):
  · Beat 2 — financial (insurance)
  · Beat 3 — brand-risk (luxury house product)
  · Beat 4 — physical (crew survival)
  Three different KINDS. ✓

SIZE CHECK (Rule 2):
  · Beat 4 = the loudest visual (predator stare on a polar bear)
  · Beat 5 = the smallest (pull-back + wordmark). ✓

GATE: ☑ all 4 reasons / question slots filled · ☑ all 5 visuals filled
      ☑ beat 4 most dramatic, beat 5 smallest · ☑ 3 different KINDS of impossibility
