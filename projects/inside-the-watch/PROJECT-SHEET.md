## BLOCK 1 — Concept + brief

> A 30-second IG Reel for a hypothetical heritage timepiece brief. Macro shot of an old-world dress watch face. The camera enters the dial. The watch's interior is a fully-realized miniature 1920s city — gear-wheels become roads, tiny cars driving, people walking on jewel-paved sidewalks, office buildings made of escapements. A heritage narrator (Patek "Generations" register) asks: *"What if a watch wasn't a watch — but a city you could live in?"* Audience: luxury watch buyers, heritage brand creative directors, horology enthusiasts. Format: 30-sec vertical 9:16, Path B VO (heritage-narrator register), vintage Italian lounge music.

## BLOCK 7 — Audio brief

### VO script — clean paste block

> "Some objects are too small to live in."
>
> "Some are too small to walk through."
>
> "Most are too small to drive across."
>
> "And most are too small to call home."
>
> "But not this one. AI Production Club. Studio shoots no studio could shoot."

**Word count:** 36
**Density:** 1.20 w/sec average · Beat 5 spikes to 2.17 w/s

### Path decision

**Path B.** Heritage-narrator register is delivery-critical. Veo's voice register is too generic for this tone. Record VO with a measured, quiet, weighted delivery.

### Music register

**Vintage Italian lounge** — Bruno Martino-style instrumental piano + woody bass. Same register as F.S.01. Sustains across all 5 beats; slight rallentando at B5 wordmark fade.

### SFX per beat

| B3 | Tiny engine sound · single car horn at sec 16 |

| B5 | Music holds final chord · final watch tick lands at wordmark fade-in |
