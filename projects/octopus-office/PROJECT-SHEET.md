## BLOCK 1 — Concept + brief

> A 30-second IG Reel showing an open-plan corporate office at 10am where every employee is an octopus in business attire. Standing desks, meeting rooms, watercooler — but every "person" is an octopus tentacle-typing on keyboards, holding coffee mugs, presenting slides. A dry corporate narrator delivers stock corporate-speak ("Productivity is up 23%, collaboration scores have never been stronger"). The disconnect between the polished VO and the visual absurdity IS the joke. Audience: tech / SaaS IG, B2B comedy, corporate-disillusionment crowd. Format: 30-sec vertical 9:16 IG Reel, Path B VO (recorded corporate-stock register), stock corporate music bed.

**Word count:** 38
**Density:** 1.27 w/sec average · Beat 5 spikes to 2.0 w/s

### Path decision

**Path B (recorded VO over silent video).** Corporate-stock register is delivery-critical — the joke lands when the voice sounds EARNEST. Veo's voice register varies between clips, undermining the deadpan. Recorded VO at neutral register over silent visuals.

### Music register

**Generic corporate stock music** — intentionally bland upbeat acoustic guitar (think Apple-keynote-style or Slack-ad-style). The blandness amplifies the absurdity.
