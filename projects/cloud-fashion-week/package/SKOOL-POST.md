## Cross-posting strategy

| Platform | What to post | Link to |
|---|---|---|
| Skool (main feed) | Full version (case-study-led) | Notion case study |
| IG Reel | Just the reel + short caption with curiosity hook | Bio link → Notion |
| TikTok | Same as IG | Bio link → Notion |
| X / Twitter | 1-2 tweet thread with reel + key stat ("$15 / 15 seconds") | Notion case study |
| Threads | Reel embed + provocative line ("they said you need a studio") | Notion case study |
| LinkedIn | More polished, business-angle ("AI production at studio quality") | Notion case study |

The pattern: reel goes everywhere, all roads link to ONE Notion case study, Notion ends with Skool invite.

## Engagement hooks (use in comments)

When people start engaging:

- "What's the most impossible thing you've wanted to make?"
- "What stops you from shipping AI content right now?"
- "Tell me your concept — I'll do a teardown of whether the workflow can handle it"

These prompt high-signal replies that you can later batch-respond to with personalized Skool invites.
