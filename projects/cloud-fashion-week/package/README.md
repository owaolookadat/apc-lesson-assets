## How to use this kit

### Step 1 — Read DOCTRINES.md first (15 min)

13 doctrines from this build. If you skip this, you'll re-discover them by burning $20-50 in failed iterations. **Read first, save credits.**

### Step 2 — Fill out PROJECT-SHEET-GUIDED.md (30-60 min)

Your concept. Your character. Your impossibility. Don't skip blocks — every block exists because skipping it costs more later.

### Step 3 — Generate F/E/M sheets (1-2 hours, ~$2)

Use the sheet prompts in PROMPTS-LIBRARY.md as templates. Adapt the language to your concept. **Generate sheets manually in Higgsfield web UI** (NOT MCP — references don't propagate via MCP for nano_banana_pro).

### Step 4 — Generate beat stills (2-4 hours, ~$3-6)

One still per beat. Attach the references the doctrines say to attach. Iterate until each passes its keeper checks.

### Step 5 — Upscale all stills (10 min, ~$1)

Topaz x2, Face enhancement OFF. Do this BEFORE video gen so Kling has higher quality input.

### Step 6 — Generate video clips (1-2 hours, ~$3-5)

Image-to-video in Kling 3.0. Use the motion prompts from PROMPTS-LIBRARY.md. Sound OFF (Path B doctrine).

### Step 7 — Record VO (15 min, ~$0)

ElevenLabs v3 with your cloned voice OR a v3 preset. Use the VO script structure from our reel as a template.

### Step 8 — Stitch in CapCut (1-2 hours)

Arrange clips on timeline, place VO, add music + SFX, add wordmark. Export 1080p 9:16.

### Step 9 — Ship to IG Reel + TikTok

Caption + hashtags + CTA per your Project Sheet's Block 2 distribution plan.
