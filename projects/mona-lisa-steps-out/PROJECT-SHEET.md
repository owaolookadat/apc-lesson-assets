## BLOCK 1 — Concept + brief

> A 30-second IG Reel set in the Louvre at midnight. The Mona Lisa's frame on the wall is empty in the wide. She is now standing next to her frame, wearing modern fashion (leather jacket, designer sunglasses), being photographed by a fashion photographer who's also walked out of a different nearby painting. A single sparse VO line: *"Two seconds ago she'd never moved. Now she's late."* Audience: art / culture IG, museum brand crossover potential, luxury fashion crossover. Format: 30-sec vertical 9:16 (also adaptable to 5-card IG carousel — paintings come alive, one per card).

**Cinematic minimal** — single piano note holds across the reel, evolving slightly. Music IS the setting (museum-quiet). Slight ambient strings under wordmark fade.
