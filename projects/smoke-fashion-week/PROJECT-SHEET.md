## BLOCK 1 — Concept + brief

> A 30-second IG Reel showing a high-fashion runway where every garment, accessory, and the model's hair is made of curling smoke — couture gowns that dissolve and re-form, suits as billowing volumes, an audience in the dark watching it happen. Audience: fashion industry, art / design IG, creative directors. The reel has no spoken VO until the final brand reveal — the visual impossibility carries the whole 30 seconds. Format: 30-sec vertical 9:16 IG Reel, music-driven, ambient runway SFX, AAC audio to be mixed in CapCut post-generation (Path B doctrine).

**Word count:** 8 (entirely in beat 5)
**Density:** 0.27 w/sec across the full reel; 1.33 w/sec in beat 5 alone

### Path decision

**Path B (recorded VO over silent video).** Path A is unreliable for branded VO. Beat 5 needs the brand line said cleanly. Layer over silent Veo visuals in CapCut per C7 default workflow.

### Music register

**Cinematic minimal** — ambient piano + sparse strings (Jóhann Jóhannsson "Flight from the City" style). Sustains across beats 1–4; swells half-step at B4 peak; resolves into wordmark fade in B5.
