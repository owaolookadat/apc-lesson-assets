## BLOCK 1 — Concept + brief

> A 30-second IG Reel showing a model in matte-black couture wearing jewelry made entirely of flowing mercury — a necklace that pours but holds the shape of a necklace, a wristwatch whose face is liquid metal frozen in motion, rings that hang from her fingers like beads of water defying gravity. A single dry VO line at the end. Pure visual impossibility. Audience: avant-garde fashion houses, perfume brands, watch brands, art directors. Format: 30-sec vertical 9:16 IG Reel + adaptable to single hero still for static-post version.

**Density:** 0.70 w/sec across the reel; 3.5 w/sec in beat 5

### Path decision

**Path B.** F.AD.01 Authenticity Hack register — calm, dry, observational. Veo's voice would feel wrong for this dry register. Recorded VO over silent visuals + careful SFX layer.

### Music register

**Cinematic minimal with metallic accents** — sparse piano + sustained string drone + faint metallic shimmer (subtle bell-like resonance under the music). Holds across all 5 beats; resolves to one chord at wordmark fade.
