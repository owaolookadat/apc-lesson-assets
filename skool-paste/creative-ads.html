Creative Ads — the deliverable layer

Up to now the curriculum has taught you to generate stills, lock identity, build frameworks, and produce coherent shoots. This page is where it becomes money — three worked examples of the full pipeline, from input sheet through framework to a posted ad, end to end.

Every example below is the SAME PATTERN: a sheet (character or product) feeds into a shoot framework, which produces a coherent set, which becomes an Instagram-ready ad with a headline overlay. That pattern is the deliverable. Once you can run it, you have a service you can charge for.

The universal ad pipeline

StepWhat happensLesson
1One client photo (or one product photo)(client supplies)
2Build the sheet — 5 views or 5 anglesF-Sheet / F-PSheet
3Pick the framework — Editorial Portrait / UGC / Product Hero / etc.Frameworks Library

The whole curriculum collapses into this table. If you can run step 1 through step 7 for a single client, you can ship paid work. The three entries below are the templates for the three most-common deliverables.

F.AD.01 — Founder Personal-Brand Carousel

Use when: a founder, creator, or executive wants a 5-card Instagram carousel that establishes them as a credible voice in their space. Same person, five moments, one consistent visual identity. Doubles as paid exposure-ad creative for follower growth.

The pipeline

#InputStep
1One client selfie / headshotRun F-Sheet recipe → produce 5-view character sheet
2The character sheetRun Editorial Portrait framework (F.M.01) → 8-shot coherent set with cafe trio
3The 8 stillsPick the 5 strongest. Apply F-Hook overlay on Card 1 hero. Cards 2-5 follow the carousel template.

The character sheet (input)

Built from a single input photo using Recipe F.SHEET.01. Five views: front / three-quarter left / profile / three-quarter back / hero close-up. Same identity, same wardrobe, same lighting. This is the asset every downstream shot references.

The 5-card carousel (output)

CardJobHeadline pattern (overlay)
1 — StudioHook + promise"How I built a 1,000-person brand without a photographer"
2 — CafeProblem you've felt too"I had one selfie and a half-built brand"
3 — OfficeThe reveal"Then I learned to build a sheet, not chase prompts"
4 — LoftThe method"5 views. One framework. 6-shot brand drop in 8 minutes."
5 — HandsCTA"Steal the framework — link in bio"
Why this hooks. The carousel reads as one shoot because the character sheet locked the identity across all five cards. Card 1 stops scroll. Card 2 builds connection ("I had one selfie too"). Card 3 promises a method. Card 4 hands the artifact (the framework). Card 5 sends them to take action. That's the full 5-card funnel.

The exposure-ad version

Re-export Card 1 at 4:5 with the same hook overlay. That's the paid-ad creative. Same image, same headline — but ran as a Meta cold-traffic ad targeting your ICP. No separate "ad version" needed. The post IS the ad.

F.AD.02 — Product Launch Hero Ad

Use when: a brand launches a new SKU and needs a single hero image that runs as both the announcement post AND the paid launch ad. Premium minimalist composition, clean top third for the headline, the product center-frame.

The pipeline

#InputStep
1One client product photoRun F-PSheet recipe → produce 5-angle product sheet
2The product sheetRun Product Hero framework (F.P.01) → marquee hero shot, dark slate, 4:5 aspect, clean top third
3The hero shotApply F-Hook overlay: lime headline top, proof bottom. Single-image launch ad.

The product sheet (input)

Built from a single input photo using Recipe F.PSHEET.01. Five angles: three-quarter hero / dead-front / profile / top-down / detail macro. Same watch, same finish, same proportions, same lighting. The reference asset for every campaign shot.

The hero launch shot (output)

4:5 portrait, dark slate surface, controlled specular highlights, deliberate clean negative space in the top third. Ready for the headline overlay.

The ad copy

Overlay positionCopy
Top third (lime, weight 800, 64px)"The watch that earned its quiet."
Bottom third (cream, weight 500, 22px)"Pre-order opens Friday · link in bio"
One generation, one ad. The product sheet was the only thing that took setup time (~5 min). The hero shot took one prompt + one paste of Recipe F.P.01. The overlay is 20 lines of HTML. Total: a campaign-ready launch ad in under 15 minutes, generated from one client product photo.

F.AD.03 — UGC Creator Ad — character + product

Use when: the brief is paid social that needs to feel like a real customer review. A creator using the product in their actual life, harsh window light, slight skin texture, iPhone aesthetic. The ad LOOKS like organic UGC because the locks make it real.

The pipeline

#InputStep
1Two inputs: one creator photo + one product photoRun F-Sheet for creator AND F-PSheet for product → two sheets
2Both sheets attachedRun UGC framework (F.M.02 with realism lock) → 5-shot creator-with-product set
3The 5 stillsPick the unboxing OR the mirror selfie as the hero. Apply F-Hook overlay. Single-frame or 3-card carousel.

The creator's day (output)

Hero pickHeadline patternWhy it hooks
Mirror selfie"3 weeks in. This is the only one I actually finished."Confession framing. Authentic creator-review tone.
Unbox"What's in the box."Curiosity gap. Classic UGC review opener.
Kitchen"I tried 12. Kept one."Comparison framing. Specific number = credible.
Why this ad works. The realism lock from the UGC framework (visible skin texture, fingerprint smudges on the mirror, slight motion blur, harsh window light) makes the image read as a real creator post. The same image with a polished beauty filter would feel like an ad — and the audience scrolls past. Realism is the conversion lever here.

The discipline — what makes these ads ship

If you skip…What breaks
The sheetEvery frame is a slightly different person / product. Carousel falls apart by card 3.
The frameworkStills are coherent in identity but disconnected in mood. The shoot reads as random snapshots.
The polish-spectrum decisionUGC over-polished or editorial under-polished. Wrong aesthetic for the channel.
The hook overlayBeautiful image that doesn't tell anyone what it's selling. Scroll past.
The exposure-ad versionOrganic post that never reaches cold audiences. Growth caps at your follower count.
