F-Hook — Hooking IG Stills + Carousel Templates

What you'll learn

How to take any still you generated in F1–F5 and turn it into a hooking Instagram still — the kind that stops scroll, doubles as a value-bomb, and works as paid-ad creative.

This lesson is about the layer on top of the generation: composition for IG aspect ratios, text overlays, the 5-card carousel template that turns one hero shot into a saveable post.

The strategic stack

A hooking IG still does three jobs at once:

  1. Stops scroll. A first frame the algorithm and the human both stop on.
  2. Promises value. The image alone tells the viewer they'll learn something.
  3. Doubles as ad creative. Same still, same overlay, runs as a paid exposure ad — no separate "ad version" needed.

The 3 IG aspect ratios — when each wins

AspectUseWhy
1:1 squareCarousel cards, profile gridLowest cognitive load, every device renders perfectly
4:5 portraitHero ads, feed-dominant postsLargest legal feed size

Always generate at 9:16. It's the easiest to crop down.

The 5-card carousel template

CardJobPattern
1 — HookStop scroll + make a promiseHero still + 3–6 word headline
2 — ProblemName the painSame-character still + tension copy
3 — RevealShow the solutionA "wow" still — the deliverable
4 — MethodTeach the moveA diagram, recipe card, side-by-side
5 — CTATell them what's nextClean still + 1-line CTA

Every card uses the same character sheet. Without F-Sheet, the carousel falls apart by card 3.

The headline-overlay rules

PatternExampleWhen
Number + claim"5 prompts that made me $400 yesterday"Strong claim, scroll-stopping
Question + tension"Why does your AI face keep changing?"Curiosity, known pain
Bold reframe"Stop fixing prompts. Fix the recipe."Confrontational, signals expertise

Headline lives in the top third. 3–6 words. Bold weight 700+. High contrast. NO serif fonts.

Drop over any 9:16 still. Lime headline + cream proof line. Tuned to survive IG's compression.

The workflow

  1. Pick the hero still from your F-Sheet identity-locked shoots.
  2. Decide the headline pattern (number / question / reframe). 3–6 words.
  3. Compose the overlay in Figma, Photoshop, or HTML-to-image.
  4. Export at 1:1 (carousel), 4:5 (feed hero), 9:16 (Reel / Story).
  5. Build cards 2–5 following the carousel template. Same character sheet for continuity.
  6. Post the carousel + clip the hero into a Reel. Same hook headline on both.

What this unlocks

You now have the full IG flywheel:

  • F1–F5 = how to generate stills that hold up.

Free content drives signups → signups hit the curriculum → curriculum produces the next round of content. Closed loop.
