F-Hook — Hooking IG Stills + Carousel Templates

What you'll learn

How to take any still you generated in F1–F5 and turn it into a hooking Instagram still — the kind that stops scroll, doubles as a value-bomb, and works as paid-ad creative.

This lesson is about the layer on top of the generation: composition for IG aspect ratios, text overlays, the 5-card carousel template that turns one hero shot into a saveable post.

The strategic stack

A hooking IG still does three jobs at once:

  1. Stops scroll. A first frame the algorithm and the human both stop on.
  2. Promises value. The image alone tells the viewer they'll learn something.
  3. Doubles as ad creative. Same still, same overlay, runs as a paid exposure ad — no separate "ad version" needed.

The anatomy of a hook still

Arresting subject

  • What it does: The "wait, what is this" beat
  • Where it lives: Center 60% of frame

Visual hierarchy

  • What it does: The eye knows where to go in 0.3s
  • Where it lives: Composition + contrast

Headline overlay

  • What it does: The promise / hook in 3–6 words
  • Where it lives: Top third, safe zone

Proof overlay (optional)

  • What it does: The credibility tag
  • Where it lives: Bottom third, smaller

The 3 IG aspect ratios — when each wins

1:1 square

  • Use: Carousel cards, profile grid
  • Why: Lowest cognitive load, every device renders perfectly

4:5 portrait

  • Use: Hero ads, feed-dominant posts
  • Why: Largest legal feed size

9:16 vertical

  • Use: Reels, Stories, cross-promoted ads
  • Why: Reels-native, captures mobile attention

Always generate at 9:16. It's the easiest to crop down.

The 5-card carousel template

1 — Hook

  • Job: Stop scroll + make a promise
  • Pattern: Hero still + 3–6 word headline

2 — Problem

  • Job: Name the pain
  • Pattern: Same-character still + tension copy

3 — Reveal

  • Job: Show the solution
  • Pattern: A "wow" still — the deliverable

4 — Method

  • Job: Teach the move
  • Pattern: A diagram, recipe card, side-by-side

5 — CTA

  • Job: Tell them what's next
  • Pattern: Clean still + 1-line CTA

Every card uses the same character sheet. Without F-Sheet, the carousel falls apart by card 3.

The headline-overlay rules

Number + claim

  • Example: "5 prompts that made me $400 yesterday"
  • When: Strong claim, scroll-stopping

Question + tension

  • Example: "Why does your AI face keep changing?"
  • When: Curiosity, known pain

Bold reframe

  • Example: "Stop fixing prompts. Fix the recipe."
  • When: Confrontational, signals expertise

Headline lives in the top third. 3–6 words. Bold weight 700+. High contrast. NO serif fonts.

The workflow

  1. Pick the hero still from your F-Sheet identity-locked shoots.
  2. Decide the headline pattern (number / question / reframe). 3–6 words.
  3. Compose the overlay in Figma, Photoshop, or HTML-to-image.
  4. Export at 1:1 (carousel), 4:5 (feed hero), 9:16 (Reel / Story).
  5. Build cards 2–5 following the carousel template. Same character sheet for continuity.
  6. Post the carousel + clip the hero into a Reel. Same hook headline on both.

When this fails

Carousel reads as 5 random images

  • Likely cause: No identity lock
  • Fix: Use F-Sheet — every card needs the same sheet

Headline disappears after IG upload

  • Likely cause: Font weight too light
  • Fix: Use 700+ and the text-shadow from F.HOOK.01

Ad feels separate from organic post

  • Likely cause: You designed an ad-version
  • Fix: Don't — same hook, same overlay. The ad IS the post.

Nobody saves the carousel

  • Likely cause: No usable artifact in cards 3–4
  • Fix: Add a recipe / diagram / framework screenshot as Card 4
